Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

TapMinutes is a meeting-recording app that turns live recordings and imported media into transcripts, structured minutes, and detailed notes. We designed it to collect as little personal information as possible. This policy explains what is processed and why.

Who we are

TapMinutes is operated by Abraham Ochoa. Contact us at support@tapminutes.app.

Information processed

When you request online transcription or AI-generated minutes or notes, TapMinutes may process:

  • Audio recordings. Audio from a recording or supported imported audio or video file is sent for transcription.
  • Transcripts and instructions. Transcribed text and any style instructions are processed to generate minutes or notes.
  • Anonymous device identifier. A random identifier generated on your device helps enforce usage limits and prevent abuse. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple Account.

TapMinutes does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, health data, financial information, advertising identifiers, or analytics.

How information is used

Audio, transcripts, and instructions are used only to provide the transcription, minutes, notes, regeneration, and related features you request. We do not sell this information, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

Service providers

Online processing is routed through a Cloudflare Worker and forwarded to OpenAI's API for transcription and generation. Cloudflare and OpenAI process data only to provide these services. Under OpenAI's API data policy, API content is not used to train its models.

Live captions

Live captions shown while you record are generated on your device using Apple's Speech framework. That live-caption text is not sent to our servers.

Device storage and iCloud

Your recordings, transcripts, minutes, notes, folders, tags, bookmarks, and related meeting information are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled, this data may sync through your personal iCloud account across supported Apple devices. TapMinutes does not have access to the content stored in your iCloud account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Retention

Audio and transcripts sent for online processing are processed in real time and are not retained on our servers after the response is returned. Logs may contain anonymized usage counts, such as minutes used by a device per day, for rate limiting and abuse prevention. These logs may be retained for up to 30 days.

Your choices

You can delete individual meetings in TapMinutes, disable iCloud access in Apple settings, or delete the app and its local data. Data already synced to iCloud may remain there until you remove it through your Apple Account or device settings. For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning our service, email support@tapminutes.app.

Children

TapMinutes is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above. Continued use of TapMinutes after an update means the revised policy applies to your use.
