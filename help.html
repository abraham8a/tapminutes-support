How can we help?

Practical guidance for turning recordings and imported media into transcripts, meeting minutes, and detailed notes.

Importing audio and video

On the Record screen, tap Import, choose a supported audio or video file, then select the language and whether you want minutes, notes, or both. TapMinutes extracts the audio, sends it for online transcription, and creates your selected output.

Keep TapMinutes open while a large file begins uploading. Processing time depends on the recording length, file size, and connection speed.

Folders

Folders keep different parts of your life separate. Create folders for a client, class, project, team, or recurring meeting, then assign a meeting from its detail screen. Use the folder selector in History to view one folder or return to All Meetings.

Tags

Tags connect related meetings across folders. Open a meeting, choose Add tag, and select a suggested tag or create your own. In History, tap a tag such as Client, Follow-up, Lecture, Finance, or Urgent to filter the list.

Finding important meetings

Use folders for broad organization, tags for themes that cross folders, and bookmarks for exact moments inside a recording. History keeps all saved meetings together and lets you filter by folder or tag.

To bookmark a moment, open a meeting during playback and add a bookmark. Give it a short name so it is easy to recognize later.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is available to Free and Pro users. When iCloud is enabled for TapMinutes and your devices use the same Apple Account, your meetings can stay available across iPhone and iPad.

If a meeting has not appeared, confirm both devices are online, iCloud Drive is enabled, and TapMinutes has permission to use iCloud. Sync timing is managed by Apple and can vary.

PDF branding

TapMinutes Pro can add your company name and logo to PDF exports. Open a meeting, choose the share menu, select Customize PDF Branding, and add your branding before previewing or exporting the PDF.

Regenerating minutes and notes

Use regeneration when you want a different tone, structure, or amount of detail. Open the meeting, choose the minutes or notes menu, and select a built-in style or custom preset. Free users receive two regenerations per meeting; Pro includes expanded regeneration options.

Troubleshooting imports

If an import cannot be processed, check that the file contains a playable audio track, is not damaged or password protected, and is fully downloaded from iCloud or another provider. Try a stable Wi-Fi connection and keep the app open while processing starts.

For video, TapMinutes uses the audio track. A video without compatible audio cannot be transcribed. If the issue continues, email support@tapminutes.app with the file type, approximate duration, device model, iOS version, and the exact error message. Do not send a confidential recording unless support asks for it and you have permission.

Speech recognition prompts

Live captions depend on Apple speech recognition. If speech recognition is unavailable or not ready, follow the prompt in TapMinutes, check microphone and speech-recognition permissions in Settings, and try again after reconnecting to the internet. Your recording can still be recovered if the app shows Recover saved recordings.
